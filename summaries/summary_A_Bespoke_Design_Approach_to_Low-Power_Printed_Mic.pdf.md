# Summary for A_Bespoke_Design_Approach_to_Low-Power_Printed_Mic.pdf

1. **Core Method**
The researchers employed a bespoke design approach for printed microprocessors using the EGFET technology standard cell library. They synthesized baseline designs to identify and prune underutilized logic, subsequently reinvesting the saved area into the integration of an application-specific SIMD Multiply-Accumulate (MAC) unit to optimize performance for machine learning workloads.

The study reports significant performance improvements following the integration of the MAC unit, specifically:
