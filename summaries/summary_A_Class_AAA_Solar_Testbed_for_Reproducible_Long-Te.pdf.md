# Summary for A_Class_AAA_Solar_Testbed_for_Reproducible_Long-Te.pdf

Based on the provided extracted technical details from the paper, here is the structured summary:

### 1. Core Method
* **Closed-Loop Active Control:** Developed a multi-source LED-based solar testbed that uses integrated illuminance and spectral sensors to continuously monitor and regulate the light source.
* **Standard-Based Evaluation:** Assessed optical radiation performance against **IEC 60904-9** (and ASTM E927-19) standards across the **AM1.5G reference spectrum** (400–1100 nm wavelength bins).
* **Measurement & Validation:** Utilized specialized equipment including Thorlabs CCS20 spectrometers, Keysight B2902A Source Measurement Units, pyranometers, and photodiode detectors to quantify spectral match, spatial non-uniformity, and temporal instability.

### 2. Main Conclusions
* **Class AAA Performance:** Successfully achieved Class AAA performance across a 16.5 cm × 16.5 cm test area.
* **Spectral and Spatial Precision:** Demonstrated a spectral match below 1.3% and spatial non-uniformity under 1.285% (Class A).
* **Temporal Stability:** Achieved a short-term temporal instability of 0.24% (enhanced via active spectral feedback) and long-term irradiance instability below 0.6% over a 70-hour period.
* **Broad Dynamic Range:** Validated a wide cumulative irradiance range spanning from 5.7 mW/m² to 908.9 W/m² (1 lx to 110 klx).
