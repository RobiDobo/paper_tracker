### 1. Core Method: What did they do?
* **Antenna & Tag Design:** Designed miniaturized, low-cost planar meander line dipole antennas on rigid (Rogers 4350B) and ultra-thin flexible (Panasonic Felios) substrates.
* **Integration & Operation:** Integrated the antennas with an RF diode to create passive tags capable of generating inter-modulation products (IMP/IMD) using two-tone signals (15 dBm transmission at 2.4 GHz) for device authentication and RF fingerprinting.
* **Evaluation:** Conducted numerical simulations (using ANSYS HFSS) and empirical measurements to evaluate input matching, realized gain, retransmitted IMD spectra, and performance when embedded in lossy dielectric packaging at a 0.4 m operating range.
