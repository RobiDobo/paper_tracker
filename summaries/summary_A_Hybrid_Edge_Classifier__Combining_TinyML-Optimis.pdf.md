## 1. Core Method
A hybrid digital–analogue edge classifier for CIFAR-10 (50k train / 10k test, grayscale):
- **Front end:** TinyML student CNN derived from a ResNet-50 teacher via knowledge distillation, magnitude-based structured pruning with polynomial sparsity scheduling (50% → 80%), and mean-based binary quantisation of feature maps; architecture constrained to convolution-only (no fully-connected layers) for ACAM compatibility.
- **Back end:** Templates generated by k-means clustering (1–3 per class, chosen by silhouette score), matched in an RRAM-CMOS analogue CAM ("TXL-ACAM") using either feature-count or similarity-based (distance + hit-ratio) schemes.
