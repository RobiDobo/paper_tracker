### 1. Core Method
The researchers developed a high-frequency, solid-state microwave (MW) magnetometer utilizing an **NV-enriched diamond ensemble**. The core methodology relies on a **Rabi-sequence-based sensing technique** combined with a specialized **$\pi$-pulse subtraction scheme**. This scheme effectively filters out non-magnetic noise. The system sensitivity is optimized by balancing fluorescence contrast against the driven coherence time, specifically targeting a Rabi frequency ($\Omega_{opt}$) of $2\pi \times 42.8 \text{ kHz}$. The resulting fluctuations in fluorescence are calibrated to magnetic field units using the slope of the Rabi resonance and the gyromagnetic ratio.

*   **Applications:** The platform successfully demonstrated the ability to detect amplitude and phase-modulated microwave signals, establishing it as a viable tool for high-frequency microwave circuitry imaging and near-field antenna characterization.
