### 1. Core Method
The researchers developed a **monolithic 1×8 microwave photonic beamformer** on a silicon-on-insulator (SOI) platform. The integration includes a Mach-Zehnder modulator, an eight-channel optical true time delay line (OTTDL) network utilizing 5-bit switchable delay lines, and germanium photodetectors. Thermo-optic MZI switches featuring air-trenched thermal isolation were employed to manage the optical signal routing, enabling the device to perform microwave beam steering across a bandwidth of 8–18 GHz.
