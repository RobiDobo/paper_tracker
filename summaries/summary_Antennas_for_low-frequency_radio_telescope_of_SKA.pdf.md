### 1. Core Method: What did they do?
The study evaluated the performance of the SKALA antenna variants and the AAVS2 array using a combination of:
* Electromagnetic simulations.
* Anechoic chamber $S_{21}$ measurements utilizing a network analyzer and standard biconical antennas.
* On-sky total power spectral measurements across the 50–350 MHz frequency range.
* Computation of inter-antenna cross-talk at 110 MHz using $S$-parameters and noise wave models.
