### 1. Core Method: What did they do?
* **Fabrication Approach:** Developed a cleanroom-free, additive-subtractive manufacturing process combining extrusion printing of a 7 wt% water-dispersed graphene ink (for electrode contacts) and spin-coating of cellulose acetate (for substrates and encapsulation).
* **Patterning & Characterization:** Utilized a femtosecond laser ablation system (with a 10× objective at optimized powers and scanning speeds) to simultaneously pattern graphene and encapsulation layers, achieving high-resolution channel openings down to the micrometer and sub-micrometer scale. 
* **Device Architecture:** Fabricated both horizontal (hOECT) and vertical (vOECT) organic electrochemical transistors.
* **Analysis:** Employed 3D optical profilometry (via coherence scanning interferometry with derivative-based algorithms) and confocal Raman microscopy to evaluate material selectivity, structural integrity, and physical dimensions.
