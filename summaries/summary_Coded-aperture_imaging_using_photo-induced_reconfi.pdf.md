# Summary for Coded-aperture_imaging_using_photo-induced_reconfi.pdf

Based on the provided text, here is the final structured summary:

1. **Core Method**: 
   The study developed a terahertz (THz) coded-aperture imaging and beam-mapping technique (operating at 570–600 GHz, specifically demonstrated at 590 GHz) using photo-induced reconfigurable aperture arrays on a 200 µm thick unpatterned silicon wafer. A digital light processing (DLP) projector provided programmable visible light illumination (400–800 nm) to dynamically generate spatial Hadamard-coded patterns, modulating local silicon conductivity via free-carrier absorption to turn THz pixels "on" and "off."

2. **Main Conclusions**: 
   * Achieved a THz modulation depth ("on/off" ratio) of ~20 dB across the 578–592 GHz range.
   * Reached a 3-dB modulation bandwidth of ~1.3 kHz (with a transition time of ~550 µs).
   * Successfully reconstructed 4 × 4 pixel Hadamard images that resolved targeted metal aperture shapes.
   * Utilized a 6 × 6 aperture array to map THz beam profiles at various axial positions, showing close agreement with theoretical Gaussian beam calculations.
