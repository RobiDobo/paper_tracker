Based on the provided technical details from the paper, here is the structured summary:

### 1. Core Method: What did they do?
* **Design & Configuration:** Designed, simulated (using CST Studio Suite), and fabricated a compact, dual-band stacked patch antenna consisting of two dielectric substrate layers and three copper layers.
* **Techniques:** Utilized a stacked patch configuration with a **5 mm physical Y-axis displacement** between the radiating patch layers to optimize electromagnetic coupling and fine-tune resonant frequencies for ISM applications. 
* **Target Bands:** Targeted dual-band operation at **2.25–2.35 GHz** and **5.6–5.8 GHz** using standard 50-ohm microstrip feed lines.

### 2. Main Conclusions: What were the key dosimetry results?
* **Performance Metrics:** The prototype achieved strong return loss ($S_{11}$) of **-20 dB** for the lower band and **-14 dB** for the upper band, with directional gains exceeding **8 dBi** (lower band) and **6 dBi** (upper band).
* **Efficiency:** Demonstrated radiation efficiencies between **-1.3 and -1.5 dB** (roughly 70–74% power radiation) across both operational bands.
