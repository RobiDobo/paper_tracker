### 1. Core Method: What did they do?
* **Dosimetry Evaluation:** Assessed beam interaction using a Quasar (or QUASAR) body phantom combined with an 8-element flexible RF torso coil array across three treatment angles (0°, 45°, and 90°). This evaluated different contact scenarios—no contact, partial contact, and maximum contact—using a 40 × 40 mm² Linac beam and measured the dose delivered to the target (Grays to far; GSF).
* **Imaging & Interference Validation:** Tested radiolucency and RF-Linac electronic compatibility by acquiring 120 dynamic TrueFISP images with the beam alternating on/off (or MLC aperture closed/open) every 30 images, calculating background noise variations according to NEMA standards.

### 2. Main Conclusions: What were the key dosimetry results?
* **Minimal Dosimetric Impact:** The 8-element flexible RF torso coil array demonstrated excellent radiolucency, resulting in radiation dose differences of less than 1% with and without the coil present across all tested treatment angles.
* **No Image Degradation:** Dynamic beam-on noise analysis showed background noise variation remained under 1%, confirming that the coil array does not compromise radiotherapy dose delivery, image quality, or system performance during simultaneous beam delivery.
