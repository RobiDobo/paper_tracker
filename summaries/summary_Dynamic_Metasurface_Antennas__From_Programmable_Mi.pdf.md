### 1. Core Method
The researchers developed **Dynamic Metasurface Antennas (DMAs)** based on **Substrate-Integrated Waveguides (SIW)**. To achieve programmable beam-steering and radiation control without power-intensive phase shifters, they integrated electronically tunable **complementary electric-inductive-capacitive (CELC) resonators** into the waveguide structure. These resonators are modulated using PIN diodes, which enable the controlled coupling of guided microwave energy into free space, allowing for dynamic switching between radiating and non-radiating states.
