# Summary for Feasibility_Study_of_Curvature_Effect_in_Flexible_.pdf

### 1. Core Method
- Modeled and simulated a **4 × 4 flexible microstrip patch array** in **HFSS** while folding it to curvature angles of approximately **9°–90°**.
- Evaluated electric-field magnitude, input impedance, insertion loss, beam squint, SNR, and BER for modulation formats up to **16-QAM**.
- Used the **Friis transmission equation** to estimate received power and compared curved-array performance with the unfolded configuration.

### 2. Main Conclusions
**RF performance:**
- Insertion loss remained **≤7 dB** over the examined bending angles.
- Received SNR stayed within approximately **2 dB** of the unfolded case.
- BER agreed with analytical predictions; the cited example reports approximately **\(10^{-4}\)** for 4-QAM at **100 GHz**.
- Curvature-enabled 2-D beam alignment showed minimal beam squint and maintained link fidelity.
