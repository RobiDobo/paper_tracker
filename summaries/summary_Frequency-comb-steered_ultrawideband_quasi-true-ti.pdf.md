### 1. Core Method: What did they do?
* **System Architecture:** Developed a frequency-comb-steered quasi-true-time-delay (quasi-TTD) photonic beamforming system.
* **Hardware & Signals:** Used the beamformer to drive an 8-element Vivaldi antenna array transmitting 12–18 GHz constant-envelope linear frequency modulation with orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (CE-LFM-OFDM) signals at a 3 Gbps data rate.
* **Applications:** Demonstrated seamless beam steering across a −30° to +30° azimuth range and performed high-resolution inverse synthetic aperture radar (ISAR) imaging of a target at a distance of 1.5 meters.
