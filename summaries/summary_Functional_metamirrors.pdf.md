### 1. Core Method
*   **Electromagnetic Modeling:** Modeled the electromagnetic response of metamirrors using surface-averaged electric and magnetic currents derived from the polarizabilities ($\alpha_e, \alpha_m, \alpha_{me}$) of deeply sub-wavelength bianisotropic unit cells to achieve zero forward transmission and full-power backscattered reflection with a desired phase.
*   **Experimental & Numerical Setup:** Utilized numerical simulations alongside experimental measurements within a parallel-plate G-band waveguide (operating around 5 GHz) equipped with a vector network analyzer. 
*   **Measurement Technique:** Deployed a movable vertical coaxial probe beneath copper-wire metamirror arrays/meshes to measure near-field transmission ($S_{21}$) and spatial electric field distributions, isolating reflected waves by subtracting incident fields from total measured fields.
