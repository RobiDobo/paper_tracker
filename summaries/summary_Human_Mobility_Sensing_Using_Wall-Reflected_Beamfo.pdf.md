### Structured Summary

**1. Core Method**  
The researchers developed a device-free human mobility sensing system using a **wall-reflected, beamformed 60.8 GHz continuous-wave (CW) mmWave link**. They monitored the normalized baseband I/Q RMS power proxy ($P(t)/P_0$) sampled at ~600 Hz and binned into 10 ms intervals. From 144 crossing events collected from three participants, they extracted features including **central fade duration (W60)** and **pre/post-fade asymmetries (AR, AS)**. Classification of walking pace (binary and three-class) and travel direction was performed using **logistic regression under a leave-one-participant-out (LOPO) validation scheme**.
