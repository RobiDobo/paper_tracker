# Summary for Low-Cost_Turntable_Designed_for_RF_Phased_Array_An.pdf

**1. Core Method**  
- Designed and 3D‑printed a low‑cost, motorized turntable (≈ $112) driven by a NEMA 17 stepper motor (68 oz·in torque).  
- Used micro‑stepping to achieve a rotation resolution of 0.007° (≈ 0.9° per full step) over a 180° range.  
- Positioned semi‑rigid, phase‑stable SMA cables close to the rotation axis to minimize cable‑induced torque and electromagnetic scattering, providing smoother rotation and better leveling for whole‑array AEP measurements.

**2. Main Conclusions**  
- The turntable delivers sub‑degree positional accuracy and stable rotation at a dramatically lower price than commercial COTS turntables (>$7,800).  
- No non‑ionizing radiation dosimetry methods, dose metrics, or related experimental results are presented; the paper’s conclusions are limited to mechanical performance, error sources (cable loading, coupling), and cost‑effectiveness for AEP measurement, not to any dosimetry outcome.

**3. Limitations / Future Work**  
- The authors note the need to improve motor efficiency and heat dissipation to ensure reliable operation during extended RF exposure or dosimetry applications.  
- Future designs should address cable‑induced torque and scattering, power consumption, and thermal management to enhance reliability for long‑duration or high‑power RF scenarios.
