### 2. Main Conclusions (including Piezoelectric/Dosimetry-relevant Parameters)
* **Morphological & Strain Transitions:** Sputtering pressure and argon flow rate dictate transitions from dense columnar films to isolated nanocolumns (via self-shadowing and limited adatom mobility), while atomic peening induces $c$-axis tensile strain (in-plane compressive strain).
* **Polarity Determination:** A phase transition occurs from O-polar columnar films (low pressure) to Zn-polar nanocolumns (high pressure), which can be further modulated by substrate pre-annealing temperatures controlling surface silanol groups and water desorption.
* **Frequency Stability:** The effective piezoelectric coefficient ($d_{33,eff}$) remained stable across the physiological low-frequency band (1–150 Hz), highlighting its compatibility with wearable and biomedical sensing.
* **Performance Limitations:** Piezoelectric performance is fundamentally limited by a leakage-induced charge screening mechanism. Increased electrical leakage, higher conductivity, and impurity incorporation significantly screen piezoelectric charges, leading to scattered and lower $d_{33,eff}$ values (particularly in Zn-polar surfaces compared to O-polar ones).
