### 1. Core Method
* **Design & Simulation:** Developed and modeled a compact Vivaldi-type feed using CST Microwave Studio, featuring an oversized backshort ($\approx 0.15\lambda_0$) and grounded microstrip baluns.
* **Target Application:** Optimized the feed for deep prime-focus dishes ($f/D = 0.21$) to under-illuminate the reflector, suppressing ground spillover, mutual coupling (crosstalk), and backward hemisphere radiation.
* **Evaluation Metrics:** Analyzed electromagnetic performance across a broad 5:1 frequency ratio ($0.3\text{--}1.5\text{ GHz}$), assessing return loss/reflection coefficients ($<-10\text{ dB}$), aperture efficiency ($\eta_A$), root-mean-square (RMS) current/E-field distributions, noise figure matching, and intrinsic cross-polarization ratios ($\text{IXR} \ge 15\text{ dB}$).

### 2. Main Conclusions
* **System Noise Performance:** Material and dielectric losses were minimal, contributing only $1\text{--}4\text{ K}$ ($0.014\text{--}0.058\text{ dB}$) to system temperature. 
* **Spillover Contributions:** Ground spillover contributed $2\text{--}3\text{ K}$ at zenith, rising to $10\text{--}14\text{ K}$ at a $30^\circ$ tilt.
* **Overall Target Achievement:** Maintained an ambient on-sky system temperature ($T_{\text{sys}}$) below $30\text{ K}$ across nearly the entire operational band, successfully meeting CHORD telescope specifications.
* **Aperture & Beam Performance:** Achieved an aperture efficiency between $0.4$ and $0.6$, with anechoic chamber measurements validating simulated E-field beam patterns and reflection coefficients.
