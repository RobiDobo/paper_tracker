# Summary for Magnetically_Guided_Sequential_Multi-Filler_Alignm.pdf

Based on the technical details provided, here is the structured summary:

1. **Core Method**
   The study utilizes a hierarchical "align-before-blend" (AB) process to fabricate magnetically aligned nickel microparticle scaffolds. These scaffolds are subsequently infiltrated with liquid metal (LM) to effectively bridge the gaps between particles, creating a composite material designed for improved thermal transport.

2. **Main Conclusions**
   * **Key Results:** The researchers achieved an enhanced through-plane thermal conductivity of 7.5–9.0 W/(m·K) while successfully maintaining electrical insulation.
   * **Optimal Parameters:** Micro-CT and power spectral density (PSD) analysis identified 5 µm particles as the optimal size, providing the best balance between structural anisotropy (a PSD ratio of 22) and the suppression of unwanted lateral electrical connectivity.
