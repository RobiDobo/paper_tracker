1. **Core Method**  
   The authors used HFSS simulations to evaluate co-located sub-6 GHz and mmWave patch antenna arrays. Specifically, a mmWave patch array was placed in front of a single sub-6 GHz patch, and the study examined inter-array spacing (e.g., \(h_a = 10\) mm), element dimensions, resonant frequencies, return loss, realized gain, half-power beamwidth (HPBW), mutual gain reduction, and back-lobe behavior.

2. **Main Conclusions / Dosimetry Results**
