   The researchers evaluated over-the-air radio frequency (RF) transmissions in the shortwave band at 24.9 MHz using an Icom IC-7300 transmitter and an Airspy HF+ Discovery receiver. These signals were then classified using convolutional neural networks for specific emitter identification (RF fingerprinting).
