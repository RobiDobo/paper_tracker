### 1. Core Method
* **Measurement Setup:** Evaluated the electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding effectiveness (SE) of liquid metal-based composites (including the SIS-GAIMC composite and EGaIn inclusion) using a vector network analyzer (specifically a Rohde & Schwarz ZVB20 VNA) with a two-port transmission waveguide approach.
* **Frequency Range:** Tested in the X-band frequency range across 8–12 GHz.
* **Material & Testing:** Assessed a 170 µm-thick liquid metal intermetallic composite under static conditions and extreme mechanical deformation (up to 200% mechanical strain) to observe its performance as a wearable electromagnetic shield (e.g., blocking Bluetooth and wireless signals).

### 2. Main Conclusions
* **Shielding Effectiveness:** The composites achieved exceptional EMI SE values, exceeding 48 dB, increasing to 55 dB with EGaIn inclusion, and reaching over 75 dB (specifically up to 74.8 dB) overall.
* **Standard Comparison:** The performance vastly surpasses commercial materials (~20 dB) and standard military requirements (>60 dB).
* **Strain Resilience:** The shielding performance remained remarkably robust and even increased under mechanical strain up to 200%, which is attributed to the enhanced formation of conductive networks within the material.
* **Practical Application:** Successfully functioned as a wearable electromagnetic shield capable of blocking Bluetooth communication signals to protect against environmental electromagnetic fields.
