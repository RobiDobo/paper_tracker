### 1. Core Method
The researchers developed an **etchless thin-film lithium niobate (LN) electric-field sensor** that leverages **quasi-bound states in the continuum (quasi-BIC)** resonances. The device architecture consists of a hybrid system integrating a periodic $SiO_2$ grating and an $Au$ back-reflector to achieve high-quality factor ($Q=2050$) resonances. By utilizing the electro-optic effect of the LN layer, the sensor achieves strong localized optical field confinement, which enhances the interaction between incident light and external electric fields. Performance was validated through finite-element methods and FDTD simulations.

### 2. Main Conclusions
The study confirms that the proposed sensor offers high-precision, non-ionizing electromagnetic field sensing capabilities with the following performance metrics:
*   **Sensitivity:** A high tuning sensitivity of 40.8 nm/V.
*   **Detection Limits:** Capable of detecting electric field amplitudes as low as 18.91 mV to 24.5 mV.
*   **Bandwidth:** A projected RF bandwidth exceeding 154 GHz.
*   **Key Finding:** The integration of the quasi-BIC resonance mechanism significantly improves sensitivity and localized field confinement compared to traditional structures, making it highly effective for high-precision electric-field measurement.
