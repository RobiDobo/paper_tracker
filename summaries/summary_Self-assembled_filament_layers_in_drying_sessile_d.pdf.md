*   **Color-Gradient Lattice Boltzmann (CGLB) Method:** Used to model the fluid dynamics and the liquid-vapor phase interface.

### 2. Main Conclusions
*   **Morphological Impact of Evaporation:** Reaction-limited evaporation is superior for creating homogeneous, uniform deposits. Conversely, diffusion-limited evaporation leads to "coffee-ring" patterns characterized by vapor shielding and significant angular anisotropy.

*   **Conductivity Scaling:** The electrical conductivity of the resulting filament networks follows a power-law scaling relationship near the percolation threshold. The specific exponents of this scaling are dictated by both the stiffness of the filaments and the prevailing evaporation regime.
