# Summary for Superdirective_Mixed-Multipole-based_Unidirectiona.pdf

**1. Core Method**  
The authors designed and optimized a **superdirective spherical lens antenna** composed of concentric low- and high-permittivity dielectric shells excited by a **mixed-multipole source (electric dipole + magnetic dipole)**. They employed an **in-house genetic algorithm (GA)** (population 2000, 1200 generations) to maximize peak directivity (Dmax) and front-to-back ratio (FTBR). The designs were modeled analytically using **vector spherical wave expansion (VSWE)** and scattering matrices, and validated via **full-wave HFSS simulations** at 10 GHz for subwavelength spheres (8.5–12 mm diameter).
