### 1. Core Method
The study focuses on the fabrication and characterization of polymer blend films consisting of **Poly(lactic acid) (PLA)** and **Poly(vinylidenefluoride-trifluoroethylene) [P(VDF-TrFE)]**. 
*   **Fabrication:** Films were produced via solution casting using N,N-Dimethylformamide (DMF) as a solvent, followed by a thermal treatment (baking at 140 °C for 2 hours) to achieve a final thickness of 40–45 µm.
*   **Characterization:** The researchers evaluated the structural, thermal, and mechanical properties of various blend ratios using:
    *   **XRD and ATR-FTIR:** To analyze phase composition and molecular structure.
    *   **FE-SEM:** To observe morphological features.
    *   **DSC:** To determine the degree of crystallinity ($\chi_C$) and thermal transition behaviors.
    *   **Tensile Testing:** Performed according to ISO-527-3 standards to assess mechanical strength and toughness.

### 2. Main Conclusions

    *   **50:50 (P(VDF-TrFE):PLA):** Provides the optimal balance between mechanical strength and material toughness, while simultaneously maximizing the electroactive $\beta$-phase fraction of the P(VDF-TrFE).
