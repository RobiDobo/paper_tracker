The researchers employed a **Monte Carlo uncertainty analysis** to evaluate and refine the operational parameters and calibration procedures of the Plasma Impedance Probe (PIP). *   **Measurement Thresholds:** The study established that the probe’s upper density limit is dictated by **sheath resonance** phenomena rather than the plasma frequency itself.
*   **Performance Optimization:** The implementation of the multi-stage calibration process resulted in a **significant improvement in the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR)**, leading to more reliable plasma diagnostics.
*   **Error Mitigation:** The research successfully identified the necessity of accounting for specific electronic and geometric artifacts (transmission line phase shifts and stem effects) to ensure data accuracy.
